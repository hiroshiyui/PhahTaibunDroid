Hello, I'm Phah Taibun Droid (拍台文), a keyboard for writing Taigi (台語, Taiwanese Hokkien).

Type in POJ (Pe̍h-ōe-jī) or Tâi-lô, or mix them freely, and I'll give you Hàn-lô (漢羅) or properly tone-marked romanization.

* No tone marks needed: type "taigi" and pick 台語 or Tâi-gí. Tone numbers ("tai5gi2") narrow the candidates when you want them to.

* No syllable separators needed: "lihokamsia" becomes 你好 感謝, and you can convert it piece by piece.

* Tap the input bar, or swipe the candidate bar up or down, to switch between Hàn-lô and romanization at any time.

* Not sure of the spelling? Turn on fuzzy matching for omitted nasals or final stops, mixed-up codas, aspiration, accent variants, and Mandarin-pinyin habits, so "dageho" can still find 逐家好.

* I learn which words you choose, but only on your device. I don't have Internet access, and nothing you type ever leaves your phone.

* My interface speaks English, Traditional Chinese and Taigi.

I'm still early in development, so expect rough edges. My dictionary comes from the ChhoeTaigi project (台文華文線頂辭典, CC BY-SA 4.0; iTaigi 華台對照典, CC0).
