# Privacy Policy 隱私權政策

Phah Tâi-bûn Droid (拍台文) is a free and open-source Taigi keyboard by YOU, Hui-Hong. This policy describes what the app does with your data. In short: **nothing you type ever leaves your device.**

拍台文（Phah Tâi-bûn Droid）是由 YOU, Hui-Hong 開發的自由開源台語鍵盤。本政策說明本程式如何處理您的資料。簡而言之：**您輸入的任何內容都不會離開您的裝置。**

## No data collection 不收集任何資料

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It does not request Internet access, contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries, and does not log what you type.

本程式不會收集、傳送、販售或分享任何個人資料。本程式不要求網路存取權限，不含任何分析、廣告或當機回報程式庫，也不會記錄您輸入的內容。

## Data stored on your device 儲存在裝置上的資料

- **Settings** — your choices of romanization system, default output, fuzzy-matching rules and language.
- **Word history** — how often you pick each candidate, and which word you picked right after which, so frequently used words and word pairs are suggested first. It is not recorded in fields that request incognito input (for example, private browsing), and fields such as passwords bypass the keyboard's conversion entirely.

Both are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. They are excluded from cloud backups (such as Google Drive backup); they may be copied only by a direct device-to-device transfer that you start when setting up a new device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes them.

- **設定**——您選擇的羅馬字系統、預設輸出方式、模糊比對規則與介面語言。
- **選字紀錄**——您選用各個候選詞的次數，以及在哪個詞之後選了哪個詞，讓常用的詞與詞組優先出現。在要求無痕輸入的欄位（例如無痕瀏覽）中不會記錄；密碼等欄位則完全不經過鍵盤的轉換。

以上資料都保存在本程式的私有儲存空間，其他程式無法讀取。這些資料不會納入雲端備份（例如 Google 雲端硬碟備份），只有在您設定新裝置時自行進行「裝置對裝置」直接傳輸，才可能被複製過去。清除本程式的儲存空間或解除安裝本程式，即會將其刪除。
